Privacy Policy

Last updated July 2026

This page explains, in plain language, what information this website collects and how it is used. Jack Albert Weddings is a wedding videography business based in the Ottawa Valley, Ontario. If you have any questions, email jack@jackalbertweddings.ca.

What I collect

Information you give me. When you fill out the inquiry form or book a call, you may share your name, email, phone number, wedding date, venue or location, and anything you write in your message. I use this only to reply to you, prepare a quote, and plan your film.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, this site uses cookies and similar technology to understand how visitors find and use the pages, so I can improve the site and reach couples who may be planning a wedding.

The tools this site uses

  • Formspree delivers your inquiry form to my email.
  • Calendly lets you book a call.
  • YouTube (in privacy-enhanced mode) plays the wedding films.
  • The Meta (Facebook and Instagram) Pixel helps me understand visits and show relevant ads to people who have visited this site. Any personal details it uses are hashed (scrambled) before they are sent to Meta, and sensitive information is never sent.

How your information is used

To respond to your inquiry, prepare quotes, deliver your wedding film, improve the website, and occasionally advertise to people who may be interested. I do not sell your personal information, and I share it only with the service providers above that keep the business running.

Your choices

You can control cookies through your browser or device settings, and you can opt out of personalized ads in your Meta ad settings. You can also email me any time to ask what information I hold about you or to have it deleted.

Updates

I may update this page from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
