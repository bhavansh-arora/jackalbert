About Me

Hi, I’m Jack.

Wedding filmmaker, Ottawa Valley local, and the one person behind the camera at every wedding I film.

My Story

I picked up a camera at eighteen to make short films, and I never stopped.

Weddings pulled me in for the same reason I love filmmaking: each one is a real story, with real people, worth telling well.

I’m from the Ottawa Valley, so its lakes, farms, and back roads feel like home. It’s my favourite place to film, and I know how to make a country wedding look every bit as cinematic as the day felt.

That’s what all of this is for. A film that brings the day back, and lets you feel everything you felt, every time you watch it.

Jack

Why Couples Choose Me
