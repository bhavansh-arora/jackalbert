On the Day

Present, never in the way.

I’ve been telling stories on film since I was eighteen, and I bring that to every wedding. My style is cinematic and documentary: natural, emotional, led by light.

Your vows and speeches deserve to sound as good as they look, so audio gets as much care as the picture. And because it’s just me, you get one familiar face all day, not a rotating crew.

{{quote:madison}}
