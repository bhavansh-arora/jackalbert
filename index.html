The Approach

Cinematic, honest, quietly yours.

Natural and emotional, led by light, with a heavy focus on clean audio. Never staged or generic, always built around how your day actually felt.

01 / 03

01 — The Approach

Led by light.

A strong eye for light and how to shape it, so every scene feels soft, warm and true to the moment, whether it’s a church at noon or a lakeshore at dusk.

02 — The Approach

Obsessed with sound.

Your vows, your speeches, a parent’s laugh. Clean audio is what brings the day rushing back, so I’m close to obsessive about it.

03 — The Approach

Mostly invisible.

Calm, professional and in the background, catching real moments as they happen, with light direction only when it helps. The day stays yours.

The Process

From first hello to final film.

A calm, considered experience shaped around the people and moments that matter most to you.

The full experience

01Inquire

Share your date, venue, and what matters most. You’ll get a quick text so you know it arrived, then a reply by email within 24 hours.

02Connect

A relaxed conversation to make sure we’re the right fit. Tell me about the two of you, your people, and the moments you can’t imagine missing.

03Plan your story

We shape the timeline and filming plan around your people and priorities, leaving buffer where it counts so nothing gets rushed.

04Your wedding day

Calm direction when it helps, with space for the real moments to unfold. I stay professional and mostly invisible.

05Relive it

Your finished film arrives in six to eight weeks, in a private 4K gallery that’s yours to download, keep and share.
