The Approach

Cinematic, documentary, and quietly yours.

My films are natural and emotional, led by light, with a heavy focus on clean audio. Never staged or generic, always built around how your day actually felt.

i.

Led by light

A strong eye for light and how to shape it, so every scene feels soft, warm and true to the moment, whether it’s a church at noon or a lakeshore at dusk.

ii.

Obsessed with sound

Your vows, your speeches, a parent’s laugh. Clean audio is the part of a film that brings the day rushing back, so I’m close to obsessive about it.

iii.

Mostly invisible

Calm, professional and in the background, catching real moments as they happen, with light direction only when it helps. The day stays yours.
