How much does a wedding videographer cost?

Films start at $1,950. Most couples choose the Classic Collection at $2,750 (10 hours, a feature film and a social teaser). For a full breakdown of what changes the price, read how much wedding videography costs in the Ottawa Valley.

Do you travel?

Yes. I’m based in the Ottawa Valley and film across Renfrew County, Ottawa and Kingston regularly, and I’m always happy to travel for the right day. Travel is quoted simply, based on distance.

What’s your style?

Cinematic and documentary: natural, emotional and led by light, with a heavy focus on clean audio. Learn more in wedding video styles explained.

How long until we get our film?

Standard delivery is 6 to 8 weeks after your wedding. Expedited editing is available with the Luxury Collection.

What’s the difference between a highlight film, full film and raw footage?

A highlight film is a short, cinematic story of the day; a main film is longer and lets more of the moments breathe; raw footage is everything filmed, colour graded. Here’s a plain-English guide.

Can we choose the music?

There’s an honest answer about song licensing and why chart songs are tricky, along with what a meaningful song can still do. Read the full answer.

How far in advance should we book?

Popular dates fill up, so it’s worth reaching out as soon as you have a date and venue. If your wedding is coming up soon, ask anyway. See when to book your videographer.
