The Experience

Calm, considered,and all about you.

A calm, considered experience shaped around the people and moments that matter most to you, from the first message to the night you press play.

The Process

From first hello to final film.

Five simple steps. No pressure, no guesswork, and a real person on the other end the whole way through.

  1. 01

    Inquire

    Share your date, venue, and what matters most. I’ll send you a quick text so you know your inquiry arrived, then reply by email within 24 hours, usually with a couple of questions and a good time to chat.

  2. 02

    Connect

    A relaxed conversation to make sure we’re the right fit. Tell me about the two of you, the people who’ll be there, and the moments you can’t imagine missing.

  3. 03

    Plan your story

    We shape the timeline and filming plan around your people and priorities, leaving buffer where it counts so nothing important gets rushed or missed.

  4. 04

    Your wedding day

    Calm direction when it helps, with space for the real moments to unfold. I stay professional and mostly invisible, so you can simply be present.

  5. 05

    Relive it

    Your finished film arrives in six to eight weeks, in a private 4K gallery that’s yours to download, keep and share.

On the Day

Present, never in the way.

I’ve been telling stories on film since I was eighteen, and I bring that to every wedding. My style is cinematic and documentary: natural, emotional, led by light.

Your vows and speeches deserve to sound as good as they look, so audio gets as much care as the picture. And because it’s just me, you get one familiar face all day, not a rotating crew.
