A filmmaker who’s from here

Renfrew County is home.

I’m Jack Albert, a wedding videographer based in the Ottawa Valley, just up the road from Pembroke. I film weddings across Pembroke and the towns around it all season.

Being local matters. I know the light in this part of the Valley, I know the venues and the back roads, and I know exactly how to make your day film beautifully, with a storyteller’s eye for light and a near-obsession with clean audio.

Common questions

Pembroke weddings, answered.

How much does a wedding videographer cost in Pembroke?

Films start at $1,950. Most couples choose the Classic Collection at $2,750 (10 hours, feature film and social teaser). Travel is quoted for your specific day.

Do you cover Pembroke and Renfrew County?

Yes, it’s home. I film across Pembroke, Petawawa, Cobden, Eganville, Barry’s Bay and the surrounding towns.

What’s your style?

Cinematic and documentary: natural, emotional and led by light, with a heavy focus on clean audio.

How long until we get our film?

Standard delivery is 6 to 8 weeks after your wedding.
